# Facebook / Instagram Creative Brief

## Creative 1: Wholesale Catalog

Format:

- Square image 1080 x 1080
- Optional vertical version 1080 x 1920 for Reels/Stories

Visual:

- Clean product group photo
- White or light gray background
- Several sealed Chinese Pokemon products visible
- Keep product names readable if possible

Text overlay:

Chinese Pokemon Wholesale

Small line:

For card shops, resellers, and live sellers

Button-style text:

Request Wholesale Pricing

## Creative 2: Packing / Fulfillment Trust

Format:

- Short video 10-15 seconds
- Use packing, cartons, shipment prep, or order fulfillment photos/video

Shot list:

- 2 seconds: sealed products / inventory
- 3 seconds: packing or cartons
- 3 seconds: shipping prep
- 2 seconds: website or WhatsApp call-to-action

Text overlay sequence:

- U.S.-Based Wholesale Support
- Authentic Chinese Pokemon Products
- Case Pricing Available
- Contact Us for Current Inventory

## Creative 3: Whatnot / TikTok Seller Angle

Format:

- Vertical 1080 x 1920
- Works for Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels

Visual:

- Product closeups
- Fast product switching
- Inventory table or stacked boxes

Text overlay:

Need fresh Pokemon inventory?

Then:

Chinese Pokemon sealed products for resellers

Then:

Wholesale pricing available

Final screen:

PokeChinaWholesale.com

## Design Rules

- Do not make it look like retail checkout ads.
- Avoid "cheap", "buy now", or "lowest price" language.
- Use "wholesale", "case pricing", "business buyers", "resellers".
- Keep text large and readable on mobile.
- Use white, dark gray, and Pokemon red accent.
- Show real inventory whenever possible.
