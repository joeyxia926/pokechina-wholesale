# Facebook / Instagram Ads Launch Plan

Brand: PokeChina Wholesale  
Goal: Generate wholesale inquiries from card shops, Whatnot sellers, TikTok sellers, online resellers, and collectors who buy sealed product in volume.

## Recommended Campaign Setup

Campaign 1: Traffic to Product Page

- Objective: Traffic
- Destination: Website
- Landing page: https://www.pokechinawholesale.com/products.html
- Daily budget: $10-$20
- Test length: 5-7 days
- Optimization: Landing page views
- Placements: Facebook Feed, Instagram Feed, Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels
- Geography: United States and Canada
- Age: 21-45

Primary audience interests:

- Pokemon
- Pokemon Trading Card Game
- Trading card
- Collectible card game
- Whatnot
- TikTok Shop
- eBay
- Shopify
- Collectibles

## First 7-Day Test

Day 1:

- Launch 3 ad creatives
- Budget $15/day
- Landing page: products.html

Day 3:

- Pause any ad with very low click-through rate
- Keep the best 1-2 ads running

Day 5:

- Check WhatsApp inquiries and website clicks
- If no inquiries, test inquiry.html as landing page

Day 7:

- Keep winning creative
- Increase budget slowly by 20%-30%, not aggressively

## What To Watch

Good signs:

- People click to products.html
- People click WhatsApp
- People ask for case pricing
- People ask about MOQ, shipping country, and current inventory

Bad signs:

- Many clicks but no WhatsApp messages
- Comments asking for retail single boxes only
- Low-quality collector-only traffic

If traffic quality is poor, narrow copy toward:

- Wholesale only
- Card shops and resellers
- Case pricing
- Business buyers
